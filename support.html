Support

How can we help?

Answers to common questions about MyScribe, and how to get in touch if you need us.

Get in touch

Email hello@hellomyscribe.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Tell us what happened

Say what you were doing, what you expected and what happened instead. It helps to include your phone model and, if you can, the MyScribe version.

Please don't send your conversation

To keep your information private, please don't paste your conversation, summary or any health details into an email. Describe the problem in general words instead.

Need medical help?

MyScribe can't give medical advice. If you're unwell, contact your GP practice or call NHS 111. In an emergency, call 999.

Common questions

How do I record a conversation?

Open MyScribe and ask the question on the screen out loud: "Do you mind if I record this? Just to help me remember." If everyone agrees, tap "Yes, start recording". When the conversation ends, tap stop. MyScribe writes down what was said and makes your summary.

What if the other person says no?

Tap "Not now" and put your phone away, so it's clear you're not recording.

Something in my summary doesn't look right

Every sentence in the summary links back to the moment it came from, so you can check the exact words in the full conversation. Words MyScribe wasn't sure of are highlighted. If something important is unclear, check with the person you spoke with. MyScribe doesn't include medicine names or doses, so always confirm these with your prescriber or pharmacist.

The wrong person is shown as speaking

MyScribe works out who said what from the recording, and it can sometimes get this wrong, especially in short conversations. The words themselves are still there. If it keeps happening, let us know at hello@hellomyscribe.com.

How do minutes work?

MyScribe is free to download, with some free minutes to start. After that, you buy minutes when you need them. There's no subscription. Your balance is shown in the app.

How do I get a refund for minutes I bought?

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. You can ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete a conversation?

Open the conversation in My conversations and choose delete. It's removed from your account and our storage.

How do I delete my account?

Go to the Account screen in the app and choose to delete your account. Your conversations, summaries and sign-in are deleted. Any minutes you bought go to Gifted Minutes, on MyScribe. Our Trust page explains exactly what happens.

Who can see my conversations?

Only you, and anyone you choose to share a summary with. MyScribe isn't connected to your GP or the NHS, and we don't read your conversations. Our Trust page sets out where your information goes.

Who can use MyScribe?

MyScribe is for people aged 18 and over in the UK. It's available on iPhone first, with Android to follow.
