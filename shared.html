Someone shared a MyScribe summary with you.

They wanted you to know what was said. Here’s what you’ve been sent, and why you can trust it.

What you’ve got

A short summary of a conversation, often an appointment. MyScribe wrote it from the words that were actually said, and nothing else.

  • It’s a summary, so it leaves out medicine names and doses.
  • If anything is unclear, ask the person who sent it, or their clinician.
  • You don’t need MyScribe to read it, and you don’t need to do anything.

Why you can trust it

  • MyScribe always asks first. Everyone in the conversation agreed to it being recorded.
  • The audio is deleted as soon as the words are written down.
  • Conversations are never sold, and never used to train AI.
  • The record belongs to the person who made it. They chose to share it with you.

For your own conversations

Some conversations are hard to remember afterwards. MyScribe writes it all down, lets you ask what was said, and lets you keep the people you love in the loop.
