Questions

Do I need to ask before recording?

In the UK you don't have to, because it's your own appointment. But asking keeps trust in the room, and the BMA encourages doctors to support recordings made openly.

Can I listen instead of reading?

Yes. MyScribe can read the summary, or any part of the conversation, aloud in a clear voice.

Is the summary always right?

It's written by AI, so it may miss things. That's why the full conversation is always there to check, and anything that wasn't heard clearly is flagged. If in doubt, ask your clinician.
