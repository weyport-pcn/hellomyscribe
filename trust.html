Our promises

Never sold

We don't sell your conversations, your summaries or anything about you. Not to advertisers, insurers, data brokers or anyone else.

Never used to train AI

Your conversations are used to write your summary and answer your questions. They are never used to train AI, by us or by anyone who helps us.

Audio deleted, words kept

The recording only exists long enough to be turned into words. You keep the words and your summary, stored encrypted in your account until you delete them.

Shared only by you

Your conversation isn't shared with your GP, the NHS, insurers or advertisers. The companies listed below handle it only to run MyScribe. Anyone else sees it only if you share it.

Where your conversation goes

From the moment you press record to the moment you share, step by step.

  1. 1

    You ask, then record

    MyScribe opens on the question, so everyone in the room knows. While you talk, the recording stays on your phone.

    On your phone
  2. 2

    It's written down

    When you press stop, the recording is sent securely to Deepgram, a speech-to-text company, to be turned into words. Deepgram works on it in memory and doesn't store it, and we've opted out of Deepgram using it to improve their models.

    Deepgram, in the EU
  3. 3

    The audio is deleted

    Once the words are saved, the recording is deleted from your phone. From here on, MyScribe works only with the words.

    Audio gone
  4. 4

    The story is written

    The words are sent to Google Cloud's Vertex AI to write your summary, and to find the exact words when you use Ask Your Conversation. Google doesn't use them to train its models.

    Google Cloud, UK or EU region
  5. 5

    It's kept safely

    Your words and summaries are stored encrypted in your account, until you delete them. Questions you ask with Ask Your Conversation aren't saved.

    Google Cloud, London
  6. 6

    You choose who sees it

    Share your summary with a daughter, a partner or whoever helps you. If you don't share it, it stays in your account.

    Only the people you choose

This is how MyScribe works at launch. Behind the scenes, AI suppliers can change over time. If we ever change who helps us, or where your words are handled, we'll update this page first.

What we will never do

  • Sell your information, or share it for advertising
  • Use your conversations to train AI
  • Keep the recording of your appointment
  • Pass your conversations to the NHS, your GP, insurers or any data platform. They only go where you send them
  • Put advertising or tracking tools in the app
  • Add anything to what was said. MyScribe doesn't diagnose, advise or recommend treatment

You're in control

Delete a conversation

Delete any conversation and its summary, whenever you like. It's gone from your account and our storage.

Delete your account

Delete your account from the app, and everything goes with it: your conversations, your summaries and your sign-in. Any unused credit is lost too, so use it up first if you can.

Change your mind

You agree to MyScribe handling your health information when you sign up. You can withdraw that agreement at any time in the app. If you do, your saved conversations are deleted, and you stay signed in with your account and any credit kept.

If you stop using MyScribe

If you don't sign in for 12 months, we close your account. We'll email you 30 days and 7 days before, showing your credit balance, and signing in keeps everything. Once an account is closed, its conversations are deleted within 45 days and any unused credit is lost.

Ask for a copy

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, or to correct it, by emailing hello@hellomyscribe.com.

Good questions

Can my GP or the NHS see my conversations?

No. MyScribe isn't connected to your GP record or to any NHS system. If you want your GP to see something, you can share it with them yourself.

Can MyScribe staff read my conversations?

We don't read your conversations. They're stored encrypted, and access is limited to keeping the service running safely, for example fixing a fault you've asked us to look at.

Why is AI involved at all?

AI turns the recording into words, writes the summary, and finds the exact words when you ask a question. Ask Your Conversation never writes its own answer. It only shows you what was actually said.

Why are my sign-in details held in the United States?

We use Google's sign-in service, which holds account details (your name, email address and how you sign in) in the United States. Your conversations and summaries are never held there.

Could any of this change?

If we ever need to change how your information is handled, we'll tell you first, and update this page, so you can take a copy or delete it before anything changes.

How do I raise a concern?

Email hello@hellomyscribe.com and we'll get back to you. If you're not happy with our answer, you can contact the Information Commissioner's Office.
