Privacy

This notice explains what happens to your information when you use the MyScribe app and this website. For a plain, step-by-step picture of where your conversation goes, read our Trust page.

Last updated 18 November 2026: this notice now covers the MyScribe app as well as this website.

Who we are

MyScribe is made by Opsimath Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales, company number 17461745. Opsimath Ltd is responsible for your information under UK data protection law, and is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (registration number ZC286535). You can contact us about anything in this notice at hello@hellomyscribe.com.

The MyScribe app

What we collect

Your account. Your name, email address and how you sign in (Apple or Google), the date you agreed to MyScribe handling your health information, and your confirmation that you're 18 or over.

Your recordings. When you press stop, the recording is sent to be turned into words. The recording is then deleted. We never keep it.

Your conversations and summaries. The words of each conversation, and the summary MyScribe writes from them. These can include health information, which the law gives extra protection.

Questions you ask. Questions you type or speak in Ask Your Conversation are used to find the answer and are not saved. If you speak a question, the audio is deleted as soon as it has been turned into words.

Your minutes. Your minutes balance, and confirmation from Apple when you buy minutes. Apple handles the payment itself. We never see your card details.

Crash reports. If the app crashes or something fails, a report is sent so we can fix it. It contains technical details only: the error, app version, phone model and iOS version, with a random code for the app on your phone. It never includes your conversations or who you are.

Technical records. Our servers keep basic technical records, such as when a request was made. They never include what was said, and they're deleted automatically.

The app has no advertising or tracking, and we don't use analytics to follow how you use it.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

We use your conversations to write your summary, answer your questions and keep them in your account. Because they can include health information, we only do this with your explicit consent, which you give when you sign up. You can withdraw it at any time in the app. If you do, your saved conversations are deleted, and your account and minutes are kept.

We use your account details and minutes to provide the service you've signed up for.

We use crash reports and technical records because we have a legitimate interest in keeping MyScribe working safely and fixing faults.

MyScribe uses AI to turn recordings into words and to write summaries. It doesn't make decisions about you, and it doesn't give medical advice.

Who handles it for us

These companies handle your information on our behalf, only on our instructions:

  • Deepgram, a US company, turns your recording and any spoken question into words, on its servers in the EU, and reads words aloud when you ask. It works on the recording in memory and doesn't store it, and we've opted out of Deepgram using it to improve its models. Deepgram keeps basic technical records about each request, such as when it was made and the internet address it came from, for up to 90 days, but never what was said.
  • Google Cloud (Vertex AI) writes your summary and finds the exact words for Ask Your Conversation, in London. Google doesn't use your words to train its models.
  • Google Cloud (Firestore) stores your conversations and summaries, encrypted, in London.
  • Google Firebase Authentication lets you sign in. It holds your name, email address and how you sign in, in the United States. Never your conversations.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics receives crash reports, which contain technical details only.

When you buy minutes, Apple handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy.

Where a US company handles your information, it's protected by the safeguards UK data protection law requires for international transfers.

Sharing

Your conversations aren't shared with your GP, the NHS, insurers, advertisers or anyone else. MyScribe isn't connected to any NHS system. If you share a summary, it goes where you send it. Names are removed from shared summaries automatically, but please check a summary before you share it. Once it's shared, we can't take it back.

We never sell your information, and your conversations are never used to train AI, by us or by anyone who helps us.

How long we keep it

We keep your conversations and summaries until you delete them, or until your account is closed.

If you delete your account, your conversations, summaries and sign-in details are deleted. Any minutes you bought go to Gifted Minutes, on MyScribe, for someone who can't afford them. We keep one thing: a one-way code made from your sign-in, which can't be turned back into your details. We keep it only to stop free minutes being claimed twice.

If you don't sign in for 12 months, we close your account. We'll email you 30 days and 7 days before, and signing in keeps everything. Once an account is closed, its conversations are deleted within 45 days, and any minutes you bought go to Gifted Minutes, on MyScribe.

Apple keeps its own records of your purchases.

Who can use the app

MyScribe is for people aged 18 and over.

This website

What we collect

If you join the waitlist, we keep your email address.

If you send us a message, we keep your name, your email address, who you're getting in touch as, and your message. Please don't include details about your health in a message. We don't need them to reply.

We don't collect anything else. This website doesn't use cookies or tracking. To understand how many people visit, we use Plausible, a privacy-friendly analytics service. It counts visits without cookies and without collecting any personal information, so you can't be identified or followed across other websites.

Why we use it

We use your waitlist email only to tell you when MyScribe launches and to share major news before then. We do this because you've asked us to, and you can change your mind at any time.

We use what you send in a message only to reply to you and to follow up on what you asked about. We do this because it's in both our interests that we can answer you.

We never sell your information, share it for anyone else's marketing, or use it to train AI.

Who handles it for us

Our website and forms are run by Netlify, and form notifications reach us by email through Google. Our website also loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser shares your IP address with Google when you visit.

These companies may store information in the United States. Where that happens, it's protected by safeguards approved under UK law.

How long we keep it

We keep your waitlist email until MyScribe launches, then for up to 12 months unless you ask us to remove it sooner. We keep messages for up to 2 years, so we can follow up if you get back in touch.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it or delete it, or object to how we use it. In the app, you can delete any conversation, delete your account, or withdraw your consent yourself. To leave the waitlist or make any other request, email hello@hellomyscribe.com. We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this notice and our Trust page first, and tell you in the app before anything changes.
